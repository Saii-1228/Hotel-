Our Legendary
Restaurant Brands

Step into the realm of our culinary legends where a symphony of flavours enchants your taste buds, ambience embraces you in a tapestry of elegance and the genuine warmth of our service leaves you feeling truly indulged.

THAI PAVILION

India’s first Thai fine-dining restaurant, Thai Pavilion is an institution unto itself. With a deep understanding of the intricacies of Thai culture a...nd culinary traditions, the chefs at Thai Pavilion create an ever-changing menu that represents a delectable contrast of sweet, sour and spice. The restaurant’s interiors tell an exotic story with carved teak walls, neo-classical artifacts and detailed panels inspired by Thailand's architectural vibrance.
MORE

MACHAN

Presenting a delightful blend of earthy simplicity and urban sophistication, Machan invites its guests to enjoy a diverse bill of fare in the joyful e...nvironment of a forest. The décor, the menu and even the rituals revolve around a single concept- the raw, unspoilt natural beauty of a forest dense with fauna.
MORE

LOYA

LOYA takes its diners on a gastronomic journey through North India's diverse landscape, blending flavours from the Himalayan foothills, Punjab's flat ...terrains and Kashmir's snow-capped peaks. With its vibrant ambiance, accompanied by spirited folk music, Loya creates an energetic, nostalgic yet contemporary dining experience, proudly showcasing timeless techniques and heirloom ingredients.
MORE

GOLDEN DRAGON

From its first location at The Taj Mahal Palace, Mumbai, Golden Dragon has introduced guests to rarefied, divine experiences that are unparalleled. Kn...own for its talented chefs and exotic Sichuan and Cantonese preparations, Golden Dragon presents piquant delicacies crafted using authentic ingredients and culinary traditions, in a stunning environment.
MORE

WASABI BY MORIMOTO

Wasabi by Morimoto offers its guests authentic Japanese culinary treasures from the repertoire of Iron Chef Morimoto. The ingredients, including seafo...od and wasabi, are specially flown down from Japan. With a selection of delicacies, each one of which is balanced, refined and authentic, the menu at this culinary legend keeps expanding and is aimed at the evolved palate.
MORE

BOMBAY BRASSERIE

Ever since its inception in 1982, Bombay Brasserie has been a showcase of diverse Indian flavours, textures and fragrances that all reflect in Bombay’...s eclectic palate. Built on a contemporary aesthetic and embellished with India’s cultural and culinary accents, Bombay Brasserie serves meals in a brasserie-style setting, playing the perfect host to interactions ranging from high-energy gatherings to intimate dinners.
MORE

SHAMIANA

Inspired by the celebratory tents and event spaces, Shamiana invites its guests to enjoy a diverse selection of culinary delights in a setting that ev...okes nostalgia. Creating a symphony of flavours, it brings generational delicacies to the table, while also serving international favourites with its renowned warmth and panache.
MORE

HOUSE OF MING

The first restaurant to introduce robust Sichuan and Cantonese flavours to India, House of Ming crafts a dining experience encompassing an enviable ro...ster of signature dishes, combined with true to type personalised service. At this legacy restaurant, each marquee dish is curated especially for the discerning and sophisticated diners, and carries a tinge of nostalgia.
MORE

VARQ
